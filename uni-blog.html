About Our University

Education, innovation and opportunities for the future

The University of Layyah is a public-sector university located in Layyah, Punjab, Pakistan. Its journey began as a sub-campus of Bahauddin Zakariya University in 2009. It was upgraded to an independent university in 2022.

The university aims to support quality education, innovative learning, research and the development of students' knowledge and professional skills.
